Our History
Like any other unique place, we have our own special story. The idea for the restaurant came to the founders quite unexpectedly. Whilst out walking in the woods, the founder of our restaurant found himself stranded hundreds of kilometres from the nearest settlement. Far from civilisation and any means of communication, they had to make do with a simple way of life for a while, gathering and preparing their own food.
